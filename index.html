Laboratory Methodology & Algorithm Notes

🔡

Case Normalization

Converts all input characters to lowercase so identical words with different capitalization (e.g. “Artificial” and “artificial”) are counted collectively.

✂️

Punctuation Stripping

Removes punctuation marks, quotation symbols, periods, and commas to prevent punctuation from clinging to word tokens.

🛑

Stop-Word Removal

Filters out non-informative syntactic function words (articles, prepositions, conjunctions such as “the”, “is”, “and”) to isolate semantic topic words.

📊

Frequency Distribution

Calculates term frequency TF(w), sorts candidate keywords in descending order of occurrence, and normalizes sizing for tag cloud rendering.
